# Sequencer sandbox E (NON-CANON) — the quick lightsaber weld, success path and durability (hardening item 4)
Branch `litrpg/turn-builder` only; shares the preview artifact. Never touches Hask Meadow.
Scene: `ui/fixtures/hask_r2_weld.json` (the dog has run into the shed; Aero outside the open doorway, blade lit, left hand free).
Hidden simulation, fixed before any roll (sandbox):
- The dog is inside and hurt from the chase; it backs into the dark among the shelves. Each round of work at the door: 1d6, on a 1 it
  slams the door from inside (Strength 1d20+3 against Aero's hold, or against a finished weld); on 2-6 it growls and paces.
- The weld: two tacks (latch side, top hinge strap). Each round Aero works, Dexterity check DC 13 (no tool proficiency on the sheet);
  a success sets one tack. Up to five rounds before the blade's heat warps the strap (then the GM rules it cannot be finished).
- A finished two-tack weld holds unless a Strength check reaches 15 (each hit that fails to break it still loosens it: the next
  check against it gets +2). A broken weld frees the door.
