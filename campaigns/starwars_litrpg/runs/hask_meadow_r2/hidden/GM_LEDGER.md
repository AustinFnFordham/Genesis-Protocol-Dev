## The System (hidden truth)
- A shard of a Sith-alchemical "tally" holocron, built to bind a ledger of deeds to the Force so a master could harvest earned power from others' accomplishments. The ritual failed and the shard woke on its own. It scores, rewards, mocks, and attaches itself to whoever generates interesting events.
- Cost: its attention draws trouble (sharper enemies, sharper stakes). Rewards are real but never abilities; abilities come from play.
- Presentation: silent until Aero's first meaningful action. Voice: terse, literal, mercantile, sometimes cruel; separate from the GM; text only; never states hidden mechanics; never claims a reward the ledger did not award. May comment on focused items, achievements, boss fights, important events, and system messages.
- Who else perceives it: undecided; decide by roll when it matters, record in the ledger.
