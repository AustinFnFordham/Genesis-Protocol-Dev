## Established facts this profile rests on
Nine drover-dogs altered by the shard's first awards: bigger, smarter, nocturnal hunters; they mimic voices heard over the comm; alpha Biscuit; six settlers missing; the pack calls from the treeline in the missing people's voices (GM_LEDGER, evt_0003). ## Capabilities (carried by owner ruling; provisional, no creature registry data exists)
SRD mastiff baseline (AC 12, HP 27 = 5d8+5, speed 40 ft, bite 1d6+1 with DC 11 Strength save or prone) plus System-granted increments: **AC 13, bite +4 to hit / 1d6+2, passive Perception 14, darkvision 60 ft, keen hearing and smell** (advantage on Perception by sound or smell). Voice mimicry replays a heard recording verbatim (same words, same breaths); it does not compose new sentences.

## The five at the treeline (dusk, Day 1)
One voice-dog under the first trees (about 150 ft from the ramp) and four lying flat in the long grass on both sides of the lane, between the gate and the trees. Biscuit and the other three are elsewhere; their location is decided by a table fixed before rolling when it first matters.

## Tactics (role-based, not plot-protective)
1. **Lure, don't charge.** The voice-dog calls whoever answers. The pack does not rush a group in the open by the shuttle, its noise and its lights. Coming to the lure is what triggers the trap.
2. **Isolate.** When a lone target enters the grass or the lane, the four rise. Two close to engage; two swing behind to cut the way back. They do not bark. The voice-dog stays back.
3. **Take, not finish.** Downed prey is taken toward the trees (see `downed_prey_behavior`). Where and why are undecided and must not be revealed or implied by narration. Decide them with a table fixed before rolling when they first matter.
4. **Defend.** A dog that is hurt or cornered bites to drive off, and keeps biting while it is being attacked, even on a downed foe.
5. **Morale.** A dog reduced to half HP or less withdraws to the trees. If two dogs are bloodied, the group falls back and the calling resumes later. They are not suicidal.
6. **Light and noise**: no established effect. If the player tests it, roll honestly; do not invent a weakness.

## Fair warning (what Aero can perceive, put it in the turn context `notes`)
The voice repeats itself word for word. The gate stands open. The prints are wider than a hand and the gouges sit at hip height. The grass by the lane is long enough to hide something lying flat (Perception against the dogs' Stealth, Dexterity +2, when within about 60 ft). The shuttle ramp is a 6-ft-wide chokepoint where one attacker at a time can reach someone standing at its top.
