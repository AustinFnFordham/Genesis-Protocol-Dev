# Starship Careers System

## Overview

The Starship Careers System is a **secondary progression system** that operates alongside your primary character class. While your main class (Commander, Engineer, etc.) defines your ground-based abilities and progression, your Starship Career defines your role and expertise when operating starships and vehicles.

## Key Concepts

### Dual Progression
- **Primary Class**: Your main character class (Commander, Engineer, etc.) - used for ground-based adventures
- **Starship Career**: Your starship role (Pilot, Rigger, etc.) - used for starship/vehicle operations

### Career Progression
Each Starship Career has **5 tiers** of progression:
- **Novice** (0 XP) - Basic abilities
- **Adept** (25 XP) - Advanced techniques  
- **Expert** (50 XP) - Mastery of core mechanics
- **Master** (75 XP) - Legendary capabilities
- **Legendary** (100 XP) - Ultimate mastery

### XP System
- Starship Career XP is **separate** from your main class XP
- Earn Starship XP through starship combat, navigation, and vehicle operations
- Each tier requires increasing amounts of XP to unlock

## Available Careers

### 🚀 Pilot
**Role**: Master of flight and maneuverability
**Best Ships**: Starfighters, Interceptors, Bombers, Transports
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Evasive Maneuvers** (Bonus Action) - Increase evasion
- **Full Throttle** (Action) - High-speed movement
- **Attack Pattern** (Action) - Position for ally advantage

### 🔧 Rigger  
**Role**: Ship systems engineer and emergency repair specialist
**Best Ships**: All ship types
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Power Shunt** (Bonus Action) - Reroute power between systems
- **Jury-Rig** (Action) - Emergency repairs
- **Overcharge** (Action) - Push systems beyond limits

### 💻 Slicer (Systems Warfare)
**Role**: Electronic warfare and system disruption specialist
**Best Ships**: All ship types
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Acquire Target Lock** (Action) - Establish data link with enemy
- **Scramble Sensors** (Action) - Disadvantage enemy attacks
- **Shield Virus** (Action) - Disable enemy shield quadrants

### 🎯 Gunner
**Role**: Primary damage dealer and weapon specialist
**Best Ships**: Starfighters, Interceptors, Bombers, Frigates, Cruisers, Capital Ships
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Precision Shot** (Action) - Increased accuracy and damage
- **Weapon Overload** (Action) - Push weapons beyond limits
- **Coordinated Fire** (Action) - Coordinate with other crew

### ⚔️ Commander
**Role**: Strategic leader and crew coordinator
**Best Ships**: Frigates, Cruisers, Capital Ships, Transports, Freighters
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Tactical Analysis** (Action) - Provide tactical advantages
- **Crew Coordination** (Action) - Coordinate multiple crew actions
- **Strategic Command** (Action) - Long-term strategic orders

### 🧭 Navigator
**Role**: Master of course plotting and hyperspace navigation
**Best Ships**: All ship types
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Course Calculation** (Action) - Optimal route planning
- **Hazard Avoidance** (Reaction) - Emergency course corrections
- **Hyperspace Jump** (Action) - Precise hyperspace navigation

### 🏥 Medic
**Role**: Crew health specialist and medical support
**Best Ships**: All ship types
**Core Mechanics**:
- **Medical Scan** (Action) - Assess crew health
- **Emergency Treatment** (Action) - Stabilize injured crew
- **Crew Optimization** (Action) - Medical performance enhancement

## Synergy System

Starship Careers are designed to work together through **Synergy Nodes**. When crew members with compatible careers work together, they can perform special combined actions:

### Example Synergies
- **Pilot + Rigger**: Rigger can overcharge engines for Pilot's extra maneuvers
- **Pilot + Slicer**: Pilot's close pass allows Slicer virus upload window
- **Gunner + Commander**: Commander provides tactical data for Gunner's weak point targeting
- **Navigator + Pilot**: Navigator provides real-time course corrections for perfect flight paths

## Prerequisites

Each career has specific requirements:

### Skill Prerequisites
- **Pilot**: Piloting proficiency
- **Rigger**: Technology proficiency  
- **Slicer**: Technology proficiency
- **Gunner**: Perception proficiency
- **Commander**: Insight + Persuasion proficiency
- **Navigator**: Investigation proficiency
- **Medic**: Medicine proficiency

### Tool Prerequisites
- **Pilot**: Flight Controls proficiency
- **Rigger**: Engineer's Kit proficiency
- **Slicer**: Slicer's Kit proficiency
- **Gunner**: Weapon Systems proficiency
- **Commander**: Communications Equipment proficiency
- **Navigator**: Navigational Equipment proficiency
- **Medic**: Medical Kit proficiency

### Level Prerequisites
- Most careers: Level 1
- **Commander**: Level 3 (requires leadership experience)

## Integration with Main Classes

### How It Works
1. **Character Creation**: Choose your primary class (Commander, Engineer, etc.)
2. **Starship Career**: Select a starship career based on your character's background and interests
3. **Dual Progression**: Progress both systems independently
4. **Context Switching**: Use appropriate abilities based on current situation

### Example Character
- **Primary Class**: Commander (ground-based leadership and tactics)
- **Starship Career**: Navigator (starship navigation and course plotting)
- **Ground Operations**: Use Commander abilities for leadership and tactical planning
- **Starship Operations**: Use Navigator abilities for hyperspace jumps and hazard avoidance
