## 1. Nonlethal does NOT key off opponent AC or raw total damage

1. **Connection:** A normal attack still uses attack roll vs the target's applicable defense/AC and source-defined critical rules. A better attack total does not by itself increase the chance of lethal harm.
2. **Intended nonlethal resolution (normal conditions):** When the applicable rules permit a nonlethal finishing blow and no separately established pressure/volatility trigger applies, even a numerically huge finishing damage amount may incapacitate rather than kill, consistent with the owner-preferred D&D-style convention. Do not punish mercy merely for high damage. Weapon/move eligibility still requires the ruleset; do not infer all blasters or all lightsabers can safely subdue in all contexts.
3. **Control under pressure (conditional):** A *separately authorized* pressure/strain check is requested only if an approved trigger makes outcome control uncertain and meaningfully consequential, such as already-high psychological strain, cumulative fear/rage, a risky specialist technique, injury, panic, or an environmental hazard. It may apply to nonlethal finishing attacks and other finely controlled actions. Not every routine task or ordinary attack creates a second roll.
4. **Owner-preferred inverted control mechanic:** For eligible stressed/risky actions, use a separate Atomic roll where a LOWER intensity result is better: `intensity = d20 - authorized_control_bonus + explicitly sourced_instability_adjustment`; success when `intensity <= safe_ceiling` (or equivalent rule). A bonus *helps* by reducing intensity; do not attach this mechanic to enemy AC, nor naively negate an attack's +4 modifier without a source-backed control grant.
5. **Generalizable causal operator:** The control threshold is a *task/actor/situation* success or overcommit ceiling, not an opponent defense. Possible future domains: a diplomat escalating a conversation despite intending subtlety; a starfighter pilot feeding too much throttle or overcorrecting while damaged; an engineer overtightening delicate equipment under time pressure. These need their own authored physical/semantic mappings and should never all become identical attack-style d20 checks.
6. **WHISPER psychological state:** An existing, evidence-backed mental strain level affects whether an exception check is needed, its ceiling and severity. A MAXED mental strain meter is the desired **gate for truly catastrophic involuntary cascades**, but max strain by itself never guarantees tragedy. Ordinary strained overshoot may cause bounded complications without killing; extraordinary consequences additionally require existing causal facts, approved severity, circumstance and pre-enabled campaign boundaries.
7. **Nonlethal example:** Aero chooses nonlethal against a surrendering opponent after an ally dies. Under calm conditions an eligible finishing hit produces stable unconsciousness normally. If committed evidence shows severe distress and the approved rule calls for control, the player presses their own **Impact Control** button; if intensity exceeds the safe ceiling, a physical error (follow-through, wrong landing point, impaired strength modulation) may cause an injury or, in an exceptional fully gated cascade, accidental death. The action's declared intent remains mercy. Fatality is NOT an automatic consequence of overshoot.
8. **Probability status:** No 15-point threshold, control modifier, damage severity bands, risk percentages or new Force capability is canonically approved. Earlier illustrative ceiling 15 under composure / ceiling 11 under max strain and 5%/25% overshoot with control +4 are examples for balance simulation, not implementation requirements. Test actor source bonuses and different physical circumstances.

### Required acceptance tests for the revised model
- Normal nonlethal finishing hit with no qualifying pressure → normal supported knockout without an extra control roll, even with large nominal damage, where the rules permit.
- Two hits with same roll total / damage under different stress: no extra control roll in the calm baseline; pressure check only with valid trigger and prior accepted evidence.
- Better attack roll never creates overforce directly. High control intensity under strain may independently exceed ceiling.
- Valid precision/control training improves reliability ONLY via its explicitly granted control modifier.
- Maxed strain and qualifying severe circumstances may create a *possible* catastrophic override when prospectively enabled; not guaranteed, not chosen freely by GM, not possible through unapproved live rule.
- Apply similar control/overcommit semantics to a noncombat synthetic piloting or diplomacy fixture WITHOUT conflating DC with opponent AC.
- Controls and effects are committed and replayable using existing Atomic/WHISPER/event machinery; no duplicate source of RNG or state.

## 2. Actual CC-005b mobile UX diagnosis

Current `playtest_src.html` has `.composer` grid as two columns above 920px, then one vertical column below 920px. Under 920px the `.dock` becomes fixed at bottom with `max-height:48vh` and its own `overflow:auto`; the ability library/page scroll independently; inside the dock are timeline, combo judge, predictions, commit, and Roll Theater. Narration `The exchange` follows the composer lower on the page. The owner reports two cumbersome independent scrolling surfaces and repeated scrolling while building and resolving a turn. This is a substantive interaction flow defect, not a request for a second decorative dock.

**Owner vision:** A truly mobile-first, viewport-constrained workspace. Action-economy capabilities on the LEFT as a persistent palette/rail, a reachable persistent queued-action/combo area on that side or in an adjacent compact strip, and encounter/exchange/narrative RESULTS on the RIGHT. When player dice, defenses or reactions are pending, the active exchange takes focus through a targeted modal/sheet without losing the plan. Drag is optional; tap-to-add and accessible reorder must always work. The player should NOT need to scroll the whole page or two separate large panels just to build a sequence and see its outcome.

### Proposed responsive layout contract, design for testing, not pixel-perfect approval
- **iPad landscape / wide:** 3 functional zones within one viewport: (1) LEFT abilities/palette with filter chips; (2) NARROW sticky queue/combo/actions and Commit or Roll controls; (3) RIGHT opponent/player exchange, verified results and Soul-thread narration. If space dictates, combine left palette and queue into a single side rail with a tab/segmented toggle (Actions | Queue) while keeping a persistent queue counter and primary action.
- **iPad portrait and narrow phone:** Do not cram full-text action cards and dual-avatar story into unreadably narrow 50/50 columns. Keep a slim LEFT action category rail with compact icons/short labels; a persistent Queue chip/slot and primary action affordance; use the remaining viewport as a single RIGHT stage for active turn/exchange. Open an **Actions/Queue focused modal or drawer** for card details and ordered combo assembly. Modal replaces one task surface instead of stacking two scrolling panes. A targeted Roll/Reaction modal appears only at genuine Atomic checkpoint and yields back to the exchange state.
- **One viewport-level workspace:** no page-level vertical scroll required to reach plan, Commit, active Roll/Reaction choice, current outcome or narrative summary. Long libraries, historical transcript and extended narration may have ONE intentionally focused internal scroll region or optional full-screen view, but not simultaneous competing scrolling surfaces. Consider viewport and keyboard/safe-area insets on iOS and iPad Magic Keyboard.
- **State-specific modes:** BUILD (action choices + plan); CONFIRM (preflight, costs and combo prediction); RESOLVE (player Roll button / GM visible actions and reactions); EXCHANGE (opposed avatar beats, verified hits/misses, thread); REVIEW (receipt transcript/collapsed details). Keep all modes in the SAME shell with persistent party/resource/turn indicators. Neither dice nor narration require another page.
- **Transitions:** Tap ability to add, reorder directly via buttons/touch/drag, choose target and variant in context, Review preview, Commit authoritative plan, player rolls only at prompt, NPC automatically resolves under GM authority, verified effects animate, Soul text arrives from frozen accepted facts. No duplicate client RNG/state.
- **Visual direction:** anime/visual-novel dueling beats, occasional diagonal split-screen protagonist/antagonist cut-in during meaningful exchange, alternating avatar-tagged narrative as observed in fiction, optional verified attack/dodge/blaster slash VFX. Motion is presentation, never a source of outcome. Keep all secrets server-side; this must build on a truly thin player client, not bundling `scenarios.json`/NPC full profiles.
- **Access:** >=44px touch targets, keyboard and screen-reader support, reduced-motion, readable typography in portrait, persistent Roll button/focus, no hidden context menus required to discover actions, no loss of queued plan when the focused modal closes or device rotates.
- **Tests:** actual 2020 iPad Pro with Magic Keyboard, iPad portrait/landscape, phone portrait/landscape, small-screen iOS Safari, touch gestures, keyboard, scroll lock, IME, focus changes, gamepad optional later. Measure whether a player can finish a sequence including roll/reaction/review without page scroll or losing the composer. Avoid inventing exact screen resolution until device inspection.
