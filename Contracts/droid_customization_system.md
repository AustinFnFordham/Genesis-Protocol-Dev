# Droid Customization System - SW5e/FFG Hybrid

## Overview

The Droid Customization System allows players to create unique droid companions with specialized capabilities, distinct personalities, and modular upgrades. This system integrates with the existing companion system while providing deep customization options that reflect the technological diversity of the Star Wars universe.

## Core Droid Types

### Astromech Droids
- **Primary Role**: Starship support and navigation
- **Specializations**: Repair, navigation, system interface
- **Examples**: R2-D2, BB-8, Chopper

### Protocol Droids
- **Primary Role**: Diplomatic support and translation
- **Specializations**: Communication, etiquette, cultural knowledge
- **Examples**: C-3PO, K-2SO, L3-37

### Medical Droids
- **Primary Role**: Medical support and treatment
- **Specializations**: Diagnosis, surgery, patient care
- **Examples**: 2-1B, FX-7, MD-10

### Security Droids
- **Primary Role**: Combat support and protection
- **Specializations**: Combat tactics, threat assessment, area defense
- **Examples**: KX-series, IG-series, MagnaGuards

### Scout Droids
- **Primary Role**: Reconnaissance and exploration
- **Specializations**: Surveillance, mapping, environmental analysis
- **Examples**: Probe droids, MSE-6, Viper droids

## Customization Categories

### 1. Personality Modules
Personality modules determine how the droid behaves and interacts with others.

#### Standard Personalities
- **Loyal**: Devoted to master, follows orders precisely
- **Curious**: Eager to learn and explore new things
- **Cautious**: Careful and methodical in all actions
- **Enthusiastic**: Energetic and eager to help
- **Professional**: Businesslike and efficient
- **Protective**: Focused on keeping allies safe

#### Advanced Personalities
- **Sarcastic**: Witty and sometimes irreverent
- **Analytical**: Logical and detail-oriented
- **Creative**: Innovative problem-solving approach
- **Empathetic**: Understanding of organic emotions
- **Independent**: Self-directed and autonomous
- **Mysterious**: Enigmatic and secretive

#### Personality Traits
Each personality includes specific traits:
- **Communication Style**: How the droid speaks and expresses itself
- **Decision Making**: How the droid prioritizes and chooses actions
- **Social Behavior**: How the droid interacts with different beings
- **Problem Solving**: How the droid approaches challenges
- **Loyalty Patterns**: How the droid forms and maintains relationships

### 2. Specialized Modules
Specialized modules provide unique capabilities and functions.

#### Technical Modules
- **Enhanced Diagnostics**: Improved system analysis and troubleshooting
- **Advanced Programming**: Better problem-solving and adaptability
- **Security Protocols**: Enhanced protection against hacking and viruses
- **Data Processing**: Faster information analysis and storage
- **Network Integration**: Better connectivity with other systems

#### Combat Modules
- **Tactical Analysis**: Improved combat strategy and threat assessment
- **Weapon Integration**: Better control of integrated weapons
- **Shield Management**: Enhanced defensive capabilities
- **Target Acquisition**: Improved accuracy and targeting
- **Combat Protocols**: Specialized fighting techniques

#### Utility Modules
- **Environmental Adaptation**: Better performance in extreme conditions
- **Stealth Protocols**: Enhanced ability to avoid detection
- **Repair Systems**: Advanced maintenance and repair capabilities
- **Transport Integration**: Better vehicle and starship control
- **Communication Enhancement**: Improved translation and communication

### 3. Physical Customizations
Physical customizations affect the droid's appearance and basic capabilities.

#### Paint Jobs
- **Classic**: Standard factory colors
- **Military**: Camouflage or military markings
- **Custom**: Unique color schemes and patterns
- **Stealth**: Dark colors for covert operations
- **Decorative**: Artistic designs and personal touches

#### Body Modifications
- **Reinforced Armor**: Enhanced durability and protection
- **Lightweight Frame**: Improved speed and agility
- **Modular Design**: Easy component replacement and upgrades
- **Compact Form**: Smaller size for stealth and mobility
- **Extended Reach**: Additional appendages or tools

#### Sensory Upgrades
- **Enhanced Vision**: Better visual acuity and range
- **Audio Enhancement**: Improved hearing and sound analysis
- **Tactile Sensors**: Better touch and manipulation capabilities
- **Environmental Sensors**: Detection of various conditions
- **Scanning Systems**: Advanced analysis and identification

### 4. Equipment Integration
Equipment integration allows droids to carry and use specialized tools.

#### Built-in Tools
- **Fusion Cutters**: For cutting and welding
- **Computer Interfaces**: For system access and hacking
- **Medical Tools**: For treatment and diagnosis
- **Security Tools**: For bypassing locks and systems
- **Communication Devices**: For long-range communication

#### Storage Compartments
- **Small Storage**: For small items and tools
- **Medium Storage**: For larger equipment and supplies
- **Large Storage**: For significant cargo and materials
- **Hidden Compartments**: For secret items and contraband
- **Environmental Storage**: For temperature-sensitive items

## Customization Process

### Step 1: Choose Base Droid Type
Select the primary droid type that matches the desired role:
- **Astromech**: For starship and technical support
- **Protocol**: For diplomatic and social interactions
- **Medical**: For healing and medical assistance
- **Security**: For combat and protection
- **Scout**: For exploration and reconnaissance

### Step 2: Select Personality Module
Choose a personality that fits the desired character:
- **Standard Personalities**: Reliable and predictable behavior
- **Advanced Personalities**: More complex and nuanced interactions
- **Custom Personalities**: Unique combinations of traits

### Step 3: Add Specialized Modules
Install modules that provide specific capabilities:
- **Technical Modules**: For enhanced technical abilities
- **Combat Modules**: For improved combat performance
- **Utility Modules**: For specialized functions

### Step 4: Apply Physical Customizations
Modify the droid's appearance and basic capabilities:
- **Paint Jobs**: For visual customization
- **Body Modifications**: For enhanced performance
- **Sensory Upgrades**: For improved perception

### Step 5: Integrate Equipment
Add tools and storage capabilities:
- **Built-in Tools**: For specific functions
- **Storage Compartments**: For carrying items

## Cost and Availability

### Module Costs
- **Personality Modules**: 500-2000 credits
- **Specialized Modules**: 1000-5000 credits
- **Physical Customizations**: 500-3000 credits
- **Equipment Integration**: 200-1500 credits

### Availability
- **Common Modules**: Available at most droid shops
- **Uncommon Modules**: Available at specialized dealers
- **Rare Modules**: Available only from specific sources
- **Custom Modules**: Must be specially ordered or crafted

### Installation
- **Simple Modules**: Can be installed by the droid itself
- **Complex Modules**: Require specialized technicians
- **Custom Work**: Requires skilled droid engineers
- **Experimental Modules**: May have unknown side effects

## Balance Considerations

### Power Level
- **Module Limits**: Maximum number of modules per droid
- **Cost Scaling**: Increasing costs for powerful modules
- **Availability Restrictions**: Rare modules require special access
- **Compatibility Issues**: Some modules may conflict

### Game Balance
- **Action Economy**: Droids don't overshadow player characters
- **Specialization**: Each droid has clear strengths and weaknesses
- **Resource Management**: Modules require credits and maintenance
- **Risk vs. Reward**: Powerful modules may have drawbacks

### Narrative Balance
- **Personality Consistency**: Droids maintain their core personality
- **Relationship Development**: Meaningful bonds with the party
- **Story Integration**: Droids enhance rather than dominate the story
- **Faction Dynamics**: Droids affect but don't control faction relationships

This droid customization system provides players with extensive options for creating unique and memorable droid companions while maintaining game balance and narrative coherence.
