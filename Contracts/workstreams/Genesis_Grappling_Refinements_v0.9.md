## 4. State contract closure proposal
Stable hold controller/target/occupied hand/creation event, speed0 on target, no ordinary advantage/damage; 5ft reach and size bound as source. Independent posture, hold, control. Explicit candidate exception: ordinary prone does not itself break hold; incapacitation, invalid hand, reach, controller voluntary movement without drag and other documented hold-ending events do. Source phrase either going down must be resolved against runtime before adopting this exception. A held prone creature cannot stand while speed0 if standing requires movement; verify existing prone rule. No anatomical simulation.
Two nonstacking dependent control modes: restraint (Protective Restraint, any posture) imposes disadvantage on eligible melee attacks against creatures other than controller; ground control (Ground Transition, target prone) prevents opportunity attacks against creatures other than controller, without removing other reactions or actions. Ground control ends if target ceases prone; hold persists if otherwise valid. Both end with hold. Control Transition may replace either with other when target posture and prerequisites legal, one contest, no free change. Target Main normal escape ends hold/control; optional control-only escape Main ends just control, no extra forced check to end hold. Source retention rule needs explicit fixed vs fresh opposing check; proposal preserve recorded hold check until explicit fresh retention contest, do not reroll opposing dice when actor rerolls. Confirm against source engine. Positional Sweep reverses legal reciprocal hold/control atomically; if resulting target not prone, ground control does not transfer (new hold only), no implicit prone. Handoff ends dependent control and spends both participants' legal action budgets. Controlled Throw candidate distinct benefit: learned initiating Martial check instead of ordinary Shove's authorized modifier, against better Athletics/Acrobatics; only against held target; if no meaningful difference after Shove source reconciliation merge or add bounded benefit with owner review. Compound Maneuvers Main+Bonus once SR, no damage, success both grapple/prone, fail neither. Adaptive Grappler / Adaptive Escape alternate skill candidates: if legal contest normally uses Martial, permit Acrobatics or Athletics (declare one before roll); if base already allows both, no substitute and option ineligible. No general substitution of social or mental skills; Force-assisted escape is separate paid hybrid. Composed Escape penalty must be named in effect contract before offering. Movement debt from off-turn movement is booked against next turn's effective movement; reject if capacity unavailable, carry no extra beyond next turn; if speed later reduced apply remaining budget max(0,effective-debt); no free movement from rest/device switch. No recursive reaction triggers, no extra reactions. Reaction budget refreshed only established turn rules.

## 7. Prestige candidate paths
R20 Grappling, lifetime >= 4500 XP (880 post-cap eligible experience, nonspendable), verified achievement and one separately authorized prestige offer; no reset and no automatic rank21. Choose one of three first-tier paths. Proposed minimum 3 distinct meaningful challenge contexts after cap, independent validation, no routine farming. Grandmaster: one linked technique R20 plus demonstrated high-risk success AND meaningful failure recovery; learn one authored refinement of an existing mastered technique, same family budget unless an explicit separately balanced prestige allocation approved. Innovator: two owned techniques R15+ and 3 verified legal combo patterns in distinct contexts; unlock Forge review of one new hybrid, not automatic mechanics. Mentor: one technique R15+, 3 validated teaching outcomes with willing students; once per in-world day enable one structured guided practice event using normal training rates/limits; no duplicated teacher/student full XP from same actor event, student's learning individually validated. Titles in-world appropriate, not automatic Jedi rank. Prestige acquisition does not bypass mechanics approval, no repeatable stacking. Future prestige tiers authored separately. Thresholds are new candidates, not locked.
