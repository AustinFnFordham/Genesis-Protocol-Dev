## State and deterministic resolution proposal
One hold has stable controller/target/hand/creation event; target speed0; controller has occupied hand. One controlled relation per hold; posture standing/prone independent. Control requires valid hold; dependent control ends on release/escape/out of reach/invalid hand/incapacitation or other existing hold-ending rule. Ground control additionally requires prone; distinguish from standing restraint. No automatic escalation, damage, attack advantage, silence, paralysis, loss of actions or new anatomical restrictions. Candidate control effect: controlled target disadvantage on eligible melee attacks against creatures other than controller. Ordinary grapple escape ends hold and dependent control; optional Main escape-control contest ends control only, hold persists, no forced second escape to end original hold. Reverse control requires legal reciprocal grip, size/reach/free hand; on success end old hold and create reversed hold/control atomically, preserve posture unless specified. Failure preserves state. Initiating and transition contests: existing authorized grapple attack modifier vs target better Athletics/Acrobatics, ties defender; retention uses existing grappler check, ties current holder. Reversal uses initiating contest unless specified. No reroll stacking or recursive counters. All voluntary techniques require eligible actor, target, action budget and prerequisites; SR/LR uses spent on valid declaration/activation regardless of failed result, invalid declaration spends nothing. Before-roll modifiers declared before dice; after-failure rerolls use new result. One named enhancement per contest in candidate grapple_modifier group; only explicitly compatible other effects may stack. No blanket physical immunity.

## Validation gates and test vectors
1. Structural: monotonic 20-rank curve, cap3620; all 18 primary and 18 linked pool placements, 12 entitlements, resolve aliases, typed prerequisites and gates; detect cycles in mandatory prerequisite DAG (synergy cycles allowed).
2. Mechanical: defender wins ties; no free hand / out of range / target too large fail preflight; ordinary grapple no damage or advantage; release free; movement without drag breaks hold per source; control ends with hold; prone alone not control.
3. Flow Reversal: successful escape, reaction available, valid hand/size/reach, normal contest; loss leaves escape intact, second activation before SR rejected; no recursive counter.
4. Positional Sweep: illegal reciprocal hold rejected, failure retains old relation; successful reversal replaces rather than duplicates hold; posture preserved.
5. Compound: insufficient Main or Bonus rejected; failed contest consumes both, establishes neither; no damage. Distinct from saber Main+Secondary disarm/prone.
6. Modifier: Secure Grip/Perfect Hold same contest cannot stack; Adaptive Grip/Uncatchable/Masterful Escape no repeated reroll; apply new result. Exact equal d20 contest with equal modifiers attacker wins 190/400=47.5%; +1d4 gives 950/1600=59.375% (exact enumeration of independent d20/d20/d4, no other modifiers).
7. Linked evidence: R15 parent, new technique R1, zero practice; three eligible units can reach R2 only; invalid/repeated events do not bypass gates; duplicate grant denied across routes. Capped parent continues eligible cross-credit.
8. XP/idempotency: one accepted event reprocessed after restart, no additional XP; same event cannot award two primary ledgers; split ≤25% with fractions; no real-world session resets; pending offers stable, multiple threshold crossings each entitled once.
9. Team/action: receiver reaction spent in handoff, failure keeps original hold; no available reaction rejects; movement debt not reusable after logging out; no automatic extra turns.
10. Integration: fixture-based combat baseline vs each build, contested control duration, damage prevented/dealt, action denial, resource consumption, party support, failure recovery; compare opportunity costs to untrained actions and other class builds. Test across target size, skill spread, no short rest vs multiple short rests. No RNG or live campaign changes for documentation.
11. Source migration: old scaffold grants nothing; verify no additive legacy rewards; do not replace runtime until independently approved. Check protected paths. Validate registry/map retrieval before release.
12. Owner review of exact curve thresholds, novel linked effects, budget, control state, upgrade ranks, prestige and balance results.
