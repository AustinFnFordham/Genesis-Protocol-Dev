## Shared progression contract
Character cumulative XP L1–20: 0,100,260,500,840,1300,1900,2660,3600,4740,6100,7700,9560,11700,14140,16900,20000,23460,27300,31540. Transition L to L+1 = 100+50(L−1)+10(L−1)^2. Do not use session time. Candidate character XP bands: challenge 10–20, discovery 10–30, development 10–30, objective 30–75, mission 75–150, arc 150–300; require balance review.
Legacy L0–20 cumulative mastery: 0,10,26,50,84,130,190,266,360,474,610,770,956,1170,1414,1690,2000,2346,2730,3154,3620. Transition 10+5L+L². New R1–20 candidate = round(legacy cumulative at R−1 × 3620/3154), exact values in v0.8. Never mix threshold versions or migrate live XP without approval. General half curve; preserve fractional fixed-point values. Discipline 1× pilot. Source-specific milestone exceptions remain versioned.
One committed validated resolution has one primary XP owner; distinct legitimate actions may award separately; setup/trigger and damage/attack cannot double-count. Discipline cross-credit total ≤25% of primary eligible award, split by actual discipline use. Zero-FP and recovery applications count if legitimate; ordinary parry is not Force use. Capped skills retain lifetime experience and eligible cross-credit. No reset for prestige. No automatic extra numerical levels. Post-cap opportunities require authored exceptional achievements and gates.
Acquisition: undiscovered → discovered → eligible → learned R1 → developed. Legal routes: creation, class/heritage/archetype/mastery milestone, instruction, discovery, approved grant. Untrained general skills can be practiced with existing base checks; restricted powers cannot be cast through mere declaration. Improvised legal combos require no purchase; a maneuver must add an authored benefit. Candidate discoveries are not auto-executable. Parent XP and per-technique evidence are separate from heuristics; new learned technique starts R1 even with high parent mastery. No duplicate spendable XP. Unchosen nodes remain eligible later; up to 3 real options, persistent offer, defer allowed, no redraw, revalidate upon acceptance. If zero eligible, preserve entitlement. Never bypass prerequisites for rarity or synergy.
