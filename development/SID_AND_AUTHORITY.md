# Provisional campaign foundations and field ownership

SID Q15 is provisionally pre-seeded causal worldbuilding with evidence-gated
procedural expansion. SID Q16 is provisionally layered adaptive campaign
foundations with Chaos-driven causal composition. Neither is a runtime-certified
algorithm.

Prepared foundations may establish meaningful candidate history, actors,
relationships, motivations and conditional possibilities. Outcomes remain subject
to player choices, causal eligibility, domain rules and explicit authority review.
Unvalidated hypotheses remain draft content.

| Owner | Owns | Relationship projection may read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soul | Individual identity, psychology, motives and interpretations | Explicit public statements and observable actions, without converting them to hidden intent |
| SID | Campaign foundations, era constraints and inherited history | Validated chronology and prepared possibilities |
| AMD | Moral evaluation of intent, knowledge, control and consequences | Existing moral_evaluation facets, without inferring PC intent |
| Chaos | Typed interactions and conditional causal connections | Registry endpoints and proposed relationships, without committing domain effects |
| Authority / ledger | Validated commits, provenance, visibility and revisions | Accepted public evidence only |

The relationship projection has no canonical field ownership. Separate dimensions
are trust, respect, fear, reliance, obligation and affection. A dimension is populated
only by an explicit committed public relationship_evidence structure naming the
participants, dimension and observation. Other interactions appear as shared
experience evidence and do not imply a score, private motive or guaranteed recruitment.

The campaign-genesis CLI composes candidate edges from player-selected typed
templates and read-only canonical registry endpoints. It checks endpoint resolution,
relationship types and graph connectivity. Era text and proposed motives require
further lore review. Before the Fall mode fingerprints and references the supplied
original rules file without reimplementing its mechanics. The original executable
ruleset is not present among the inspected sources; this prototype does not execute
the chronology game. No existing Before the Fall content is modified.
