## Identity
Rhowarr, the Crimson Berserker (approved working epithet), is a young male Trandoshan bounty hunter, Force-insensitive, 7 ft 2 in tall and massively muscular. He grew up traveling the galaxy aboard a spaceship. Intended class: Berserker, level 1; class mechanics unresolved. Languages selected: Basic, Dosh, Huttese; mechanical validation deferred.

## Visual DNA
Crimson scales with black flame-like patterns; red eyes framed by flame-shaped markings; prominent scar crossing the LEFT eye. Glowing technological tribal tattoos:
- Forehead: BATTLE, in Aurebesh or native script (script choice pending).
- Left shin: happy face.
- Right shin: angry face.
- Upper right forearm: exact inscription "I luv Mom".
- Both cheeks: tribal markings; additional tribal tattoos across body.

Tattoo glow is cosmetic at entry; proposed empty cybernetic enhancement channels/slots, with no active mechanical benefits until validated. Armor must not erase these markings from the unarmored reference. Exact glow colors and tattoo geometry are open.

## Personality and behavior
Deep, friendly voice; roars in battle. Enthusiastic, affectionate, protective, proud and impulsive. Beats his chest, puffs out his chest, grunts and runs off in excitement. Left-foot stomp signifies happiness; right-foot stomp signifies anger/readiness for battle (latest interview supersedes earlier left-foot battle cue). Before battle he may roar, deploy armor, stomp the mad foot, play a riff and declare "You will meet your doom!" These are tendencies and player-controlled flavor, not forced actions or free mechanical effects.
Greatest fear: not being strong enough to protect someone. Flaw: charges into battle without thinking. Reluctantly shows mercy when an opponent surrenders. With friends he laughs, hugs, wrestles, invents gifts, plays music, tells stories and protects them like family.
Motivations: justice, family support, glory, adventure, battle, wealth, technology and exploration. Aspirations: build an extraordinary ship and armor, become stronger, catch the escaped bounty, give his mother a good life and explore with friends. Hobbies: tattoo artistry, engineering/tinkering, electro guitar and battle.
Favorite foods (verbatim): "Chick-Fil-A Nuggets and French fries and Chocolate Milk and Moms Salmon Bites". In-world food names may be adapted without changing preferences.

## History: the escaped bounty
Rhowarr fought a bad guy using self-built mechanical gun hands which flew onto his hands. He punched the target; the target hit him hard, leaving the scar over his left eye. Rhowarr declared "You will meet your doom!" The target escaped aboard a striking winged spacecraft with huge rear rockets, resembling a Normandy/Aegis Eclipse hybrid in the player's design reference. Rhowarr pursued but the target was fast. Target identity, location, crimes and eventual outcome remain unresolved; do not invent established facts. This is a personal quest seed, not a mandatory main plot or guaranteed outcome.

Brand: black field, restrained gold emblem, elegant lettering.
