# Genesis Character Creation Pattern v1.0
Status: proposed normative design for owner review; implementation and runtime activation not authorized.
Scope: IP-agnostic authoring, validation and packaging of player characters and supporting NPC concepts. Reference case: Rhowarr. Additive to genesis_character_creation_interview_standard v1; does not rewrite any existing character, registry, campaign or schema.

The interview standard requires Atomic legality, Soul identity and visual continuity to agree; meaningful options, accurate restatement, mechanical validation, unknown preservation and separate knowledge scopes. ## 2. Principles
- Soul first means discover intent before selecting rules, not prohibit early mechanical discussion. Player may begin with a class, picture, relationship, free text, prior character or rules-first preference; preserve intent and revisit missing areas.
- Atomic owns legality, derivation, resources and actual grants. Soul owns authored identity, expression and narrative truth within its scope. Chaos owns typed interactions, dependencies and emergent relationships; it cannot predetermine outcomes or grant mechanics without Atomic authority.
- Visual DNA is a Soul representation with separate asset approval and persistence; pictures cannot establish statistics, gear availability, affiliations or world truth. Preserve exact user-approved source where possible.
- Owner approval of a concept does not equal rules promotion, campaign insertion, or permission to publish private references. Rules authority is pinned to the selected ruleset/edition/campaign and approved registry; candidate files, retrieval indexes and comparative references are evidence, not authority.
- Do not replace a character's concept to fit incomplete source data. Surface gaps, offer legal alternatives or explicit scoped exception proposals, preserve the original selection, require approval and validation before grants.
- Unknown is not zero, false, empty inventory, fictional loss, or implicit permission. No dice unless an approved creation method actually requires them and the owner authorizes execution. Never reroll to obtain a preferred outcome.

## 3. Authoring lifecycle and interview
Maintain a resumable draft outside runtime. Stages are dependency gates, not mandatory linear screens. Use one meaningful question at a time by default, with brief options, examples and free-form answer; allow skip/unknown, corrections, and return to earlier phases. Do not repeat known answers. Rephrase for clarity and confirm material changes, preserve significant exact player wording. Distinguish official/promoted choices, adopted repairs, candidate/custom proposals and purely cosmetic flavor. Capture player and character voice separately. Depth adapts to player preferences, age/context, and intended role; never require intimate real-world information. Player controls their character; GM authors world facts and independent NPC behavior only within authorized scope.
Interview coverage (mapping the 20 existing standard stages, not deleting any):
1. Concept, campaign role; species/age/class/background/rank; mechanical priorities and initial constraints.
2. Traits, ideal, bond, flaw; family/formative events; public history versus hidden truth; voice/humor/vocabulary/pressure; public/private personality.
3. Physical profile/clothing/marks; social/private/investigative/combat mannerisms; combat doctrine/handling; gear/armor/field kit/future upgrades.
4. Immediate opening motivation (defer until campaign selected), fears/needs/boundaries/breaking points; party dispositions/contrasting strengths where relevant; long-term development without fixed outcomes.
5. Atomic scores, skills/tools/languages/class choices, final audit and derived statistics; runtime and separate hidden GM ledger; avatar prompts, portrait/full body/action, continuity anchors and negative constraints.
All stages may be revisited. Relationship maneuvers are optional and not a creation prerequisite; future titles, loyalties, relationships, victories, falls and endings are not fixed. A coverage checklist records answered/deferred/not applicable with reason, never fabricates completeness.

## 4. Proposed authoring contract
One versioned draft identity, stable draft_id, revision, owner/actor reference, scope (unassigned allowed), ruleset/edition/registry snapshot identifiers (unknown allowed), source references and provenance. Proposed logical records, not new physical schema or replacement IDs:
- Field decision: field_path, raw_intent (optional, privacy-filtered), normalized_value, status (unknown/proposed/selected/approved/rejected/superseded), authority (player/Soul, approved rule, GM truth, scoped ruling, candidate, inferred), source_ref + revision, visibility, dependencies, decision_id, supersedes, approval_ref. Unknown value is absent/null with reason, not a default grant.
- Approval: actor authorized for scope, revision/content digest, exact fields or asset digests, decision, timestamp, constraints; only covers reviewed revision. Editing a dependency invalidates affected gates, not unrelated approvals. Record append-only decision history; no silent overwrites.
- Knowledge record: proposition, subject, truth_status (established/believed/unknown/disputed), visibility (public/player/private GM), known_by, provenance and scope. Objective truth, character belief, NPC knowledge and GM-only truth remain distinct; projection filters by authorization, not by narrative guess. Do not put hidden facts in public prompts, assets, client payloads or source archive.
- Evidence issue: issue_id, affected paths, source conflict or missing prerequisite, severity, options, proposed resolution, owner decision, validation evidence, status. Block only dependent gates. Never silently reconcile conflicting official sources.
- Artifact: kind, revision, dependencies, digest, storage location, approved scope, verification state. Approval, actual byte persistence and runtime eligibility are independent.

## 5. Soul dossier gate (S)
Output identity, origin, voice, personality, beliefs, relationships, motivations, fears/boundaries, appearance, possessions, combat expression, open questions and optional campaign hooks. Preserve contradictions as issues rather than rewriting. Record character-specific cosmetic exceptions without claiming species-wide facts; distinguish public identity from private GM knowledge. Present compact playback for owner approval. Pass when material identity is explicitly approved, conflicts resolved or explicitly deferred, and privacy/knowledge scopes assigned. Pending Atomic mechanics or campaign can coexist with S pass. Example: Rhowarr is a Force-insensitive intended Berserker and Bounty Hunter background; his escaped bounty is unresolved, not a predetermined quest.

## 6. Atomic translation gate (A)
Use a ruleset adapter (not a universal d20 model) exposing permitted creation methods, choice categories, prerequisites, budgets, derivations, item routing, action costs, progression, exception policy, validation and runtime mapping. Profile can support point buy, packages, lifepaths, dice, classless or other mechanics; do not force species/classes/levels if not supported. Existing Star Wars profile uses adopted 27-point buy base 8–15 with costs 0/1/2/3/4/5/7/9 and species increases afterward; not a global rule. Resolve sources by approved scope and current authority. For new Star Wars conflicts apply 2026-11-05 14:10 ruling (SW5e default only if FFG lacks data; conflicting data reconciled preserving FFG intent within SW5e mechanical frame), not blanket precedence. Existing promoted records and approved customizations remain protected.
For each intent map to canonical entity/ref or candidate issue; verify type, exact version, schema, reference closure, eligibility, prerequisite, choice counts, budgets, effects/conditions/triggers, stacking, units, proficiency, action/resource costs, equipment routing, derived values and progression. Registry manifest forbids automatic promotion; candidate records need separately authorized review/promotion or valid scoped exception. Local id and deterministic URID per adopted registry rule; never hand-write a substitute URID or treat vector hits as canonical. A schema pass is necessary, not sufficient.
Record selections versus grants separately. Derive values via approved deterministic rules (or authorized recorded random receipt); persist inputs/rule versions/outputs, test recomputation and reject mismatch. Do not invent zero pools or fill required fields with fabricated IDs. If missing rules, produce worksheet with locked/pending fields and release blockers instead of runtime. Correcting an approved array requires explicit reconfirmation. Example Rhowarr corrected 27-point allocation is pending confirmation; candidate Trandoshan and background effects conflict, Berserker root not located in inspected tree. No HP/AC/Rage or class grants until resolved.
Possession model: identity/ownership, current location/access, condition, carried/equipped, functional modes, proficiency, resources/charges, legal actions and dependencies are separate. Narrative possession does not grant starting access; resolve availability coherently at insertion with owner, never invent confiscation or damage. A blocked item is excluded from active capabilities; its concept can survive as quest seed. Cosmetic tattoos grant no slots or benefits absent validated definitions. Progression requires applicable approved prerequisites, costs and choice; Star Wars fluid-tree affinity comes from committed events, never assigned by aspirational interview. A pass requires all mandatory choices confirmed and mechanical validations passed, no unresolved activation blockers; optional future choices explicitly deferred.

## 7. Chaos relationship gate (C)
Capture proposed directed typed edges: edge_id, source_ref, target_ref, relationship_type, direction, context, provenance, visibility, status, dependencies and optional mechanical_rule_ref. Use registry-resolved canonical entities for activated edges; unresolved NPCs/locations/items are draft seeds, never ghost links. Keep narrative bond, personal aspiration, dependency, rivalry, compatibility, antagonism and other typed interactions distinct from quantitative relationship values. No invented trust/affection/fear/respect scores, guaranteed reciprocity, automatic quests, compulsory romance, or free maneuvers. Establish NPC agency, knowledge limits and consent/boundaries. Seeds can exist with unknown target identity (escaped bounty), but cannot become active edges until resolved. Rhowarr's mother, workshop, equipment restoration and rivalry are optional story opportunities; no outcome or mechanical bonus. C pass means required active edges resolve, proposed seeds are visibly inert, knowledge boundaries and owner/GM scope are reviewed. Optional edges may remain deferred.

## 8. Visual DNA gate (V)
Maintain subject identity anchors, exact text registry (literal, script, anatomical side, location, visibility), anatomy, colors/materials, clothing, personality cues, negative constraints, reference hierarchy and revision history. Character left/right, not viewer left/right; exact inscriptions checked visually, not assumed from prompt. Master neutral/full-body reference first where practical; independent equipment, brand, NPC and environment assets before composite states. Compose with approved master and independent assets, not regenerate unrelated design. Preserve source emblem exactly; compare rendered text and shapes, do not treat approximate signage as authoritative. Asset types adapt to character; ten Rhowarr assets are case-specific, not mandatory minimum.
Asset manifest fields: asset_id, subject_ref, role, variant/configuration, revision, parent/ref IDs and digests, prompt/constraints (privacy-filtered), source/rights/consent scope, original MIME/bytes/SHA-256, storage locator and expected repository path where required, approval decision, visual deviations and persistence verification. Store original bytes, not thumbnails or lossy re-encodes, plus separately identified derivatives. Use authorized private references only; never export personal source photos or identifying metadata without explicit consent. Generated portrait may be approved separately from source. Check exact typography, scar side, anatomy, equipment coverage, hidden marks under clothing, incidental symbols and design drift; request targeted revision, do not silently accept. Distinguish approved illustrative artifact from canon of all incidental content. Rhowarr iterations fixed misspelled/duplicated BATTLE and oversized hood; final readable script and compact frill are approved visual variant, not evidence of species rule. Workshop signage approximate; original screenshot wins. Concept sheet specs nonmechanical.
V approval pass requires required visual assets and explicit approvals; V storage pass requires accessible originals with matching hashes; V repository delivery pass requires actual bytes in required repository paths and readback verification. Separate these gates; manifest-only commits cannot claim binary delivery. Rhowarr V approval/archive 10/10, Git binaries outstanding at source revision; no requirement to block design work while storage is open, but release package must meet declared portability requirements.

## 9. Genesis Truth reconciliation and runtime packaging
Proposed gate vector S, A, C, V-approval, V-storage, V-delivery, R (runtime package), I (insertion authorization), each not_started/in_progress/pass/blocked/not_applicable with evidence and dependency revision. No single percent hides blockers. Authoring complete when agreed creative deliverables pass; runtime-ready only when S/A/C and required visual/storage gates plus R pass; insertion only with I and a current campaign preflight. A visual asset may be optional only under an explicitly declared profile/owner decision; never silently waive a promised deliverable.
Package generation is a pure projection from approved draft + pinned canonical sources + campaign adapter. Existing Character.schema.json requires id/name/era/rarity/narrative/description/species_id/background_id/game_mode/tags; if IP profile cannot supply mandatory fields legitimately, report schema mismatch and propose schema evolution, not fictional defaults. CharacterCreator.schema.json is an ordered step skeleton, not sufficient validator. Existing CHARACTER_RUNTIME schema requires character_id, campaign_id, identity, mechanics, equipment; map approved attributes/derived stats/defenses/resources/movement/senses/proficiencies/features/powers/actions/reactions/conditions; route equipment by validated item type into arrays (weapons/armor/gear/vehicles required; optional trinkets/starships/currency). Non-weapon never routed into weapons; hybrid mode requires explicit approved representation, not duplicate free items. Map progression, soul, relationships, visual_identity, provenance, validation where supported. Do not create parallel authoritative sheets or UI objects. All panels project from one canonical runtime instance; player view and separate hidden GM ledger are access-filtered projections, never competing sources of mechanical truth.
Runtime packaging prerequisites: assigned valid campaign and ruleset, stable IDs, legal starting location/condition/resources/equipment, active edges resolved, owner-approved scope and validated derivation. Package manifest pins source revisions, approvals, artifact digests, issue closure, validator versions and results. Validate schema + references + semantic invariants + privacy projection; run deterministic recompute, serialization round-trip and representative action preflight without gameplay mutation. A draft can be exported for review without campaign IDs only as draft, never pass runtime schema via fake IDs.
Insertion is a separate explicit owner action through existing campaign authority (not this authoring branch): read current revision, verify campaign compatibility and scope, preflight, acquire appropriate lease, use idempotent request identity and expected revision/CAS, atomic ledger-backed commit through existing transaction interface, verify readback and public/private projections. On conflict/retry inspect current state; never reroll, duplicate, overwrite, auto-merge or activate a different campaign. Failure leaves draft and accepted state intact; use existing recovery contract. This is a proposed integration contract, not implementation or permission to mutate live state.

## 10. Acceptance tests / implementation backlog
Future implementation must demonstrate: (T1) free-form interview preserves wording, unknowns, corrections and coverage; (T2) identical pinned inputs produce identical derivations, invalid budget and stale approvals rejected; (T3) candidate/unresolved/conflicting refs block only dependent grants, no auto-promotion; (T4) narrative possession without accessibility gives no actions, item type routing and hybrid behavior validated; (T5) unresolved Chaos target remains inert; NPC knowledge and private GM facts never appear in public projections; (T6) exact tattoo side/text and visual source hierarchy reviewed; altered asset bytes or missing Git path fail appropriate storage gate; (T7) personal source photo not exported; incidental labels never modify mechanics; (T8) runtime schema/semantic checks reject fabricated campaign IDs, missing stats, stale source versions and inconsistent resource balances; (T9) runtime round-trip and projections agree; (T10) simulated insertion CAS conflict, retry and crash recovery do not duplicate or mutate protected campaign; (T11) existing characters unchanged, migration requires diff/review/explicit authority; (T12) non-d20/classless adapter test proves no hardcoded six stats or point buy in core workflow.
These are acceptance criteria, not executed tests. Implementation order: draft/decision and provenance model; adapter and validator interfaces; Soul interview UI; Atomic worksheet and validator; Chaos seed/edge reconciliation; visual manifest/asset persistence; packaging and sandbox-only insertion integration; migration audit. Keep existing runtime schema and campaign behavior until separate reviewed implementation. Use the existing standard's revisit principle to audit Aero, Varon, Rassira and organically introduced companions without rewriting their authoritative runtime or inventing sheets.
